# Excursion Response Policy

After a temperature excursion, risk class A SKUs require you to place an immediate lot hold and notify the quality lead. Risk class B SKUs require you to open a quality review within 24 hours. Risk class C SKUs require you to log the excursion with no further action. See [[policy-lot-hold]] for hold mechanics.
